The Summit

Eight AI delegates. One €10m budget. Each was briefed in one line by the person it speaks for.

Like diplomats at a summit

  1. Each department head writes one line to their AI delegate and seals it. Nobody else sees it.
  2. The delegates meet for 4 rounds. They speak on the record, table proposals and form alliances.
  3. They also think off the record. We captured those thoughts, and you can reveal them.
  4. A secret vote. A deal needs 5 of 8. If it fails, there's one last vote on the runner-up.
  5. Declassified: we open the briefs and compare them with what each delegate said, thought and got.
The point

Your AI will follow your one line to the letter. Write it well.

Run your own summit

Ask 8 people in the room for one line each, type them in and press Start. It takes about 2 minutes and costs about ₹25 on LLM Foundry. Then it plays like the replays.

All departments, projects and amounts are invented. Delegates and the referee are gpt-4.1 via LLM Foundry. Off-the-record thoughts are each delegate's private notes, captured at every turn. Replays are recorded runs, unedited.
